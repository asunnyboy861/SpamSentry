Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

TL;DR: SpamSentry does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

SpamSentry does not collect any personal information. We do not collect:

  • Your phone number
  • Your call history
  • Your contacts
  • Your messages
  • Your location
  • Any personally identifiable information

How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is nothing to use or share.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using iOS App Group shared storage. This includes:

  • Your custom blocklist numbers
  • Your area code blocking rules
  • App preferences and settings
  • Cached community blocklist data

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Community Blocklist

SpamSentry downloads a community-curated blocklist of known spam numbers from a public GitHub repository. This blocklist contains only phone numbers and optional labels. It does not contain any personal information about you.

Third-Party Services

SpamSentry does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs from third parties included in the app.

Permissions

SpamSentry requests the following iOS permissions:

  • Contacts: Used only to protect your contacts from being accidentally blocked. Your contacts are never uploaded or shared.
  • Notifications: Used to notify you when a spam call is blocked. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

SpamSentry is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, because we do not collect information from anyone.

Data Retention

Since no data is collected, there is no data retention policy needed. All local data on your device persists until you delete the app.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Clear the blocklist in app settings
  • Disable any permission in iOS Settings at any time

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

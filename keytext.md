## Promotional Text
Block spam calls and junk messages instantly. Community blocklist, custom rules, and area code blocking keep your phone silent and safe.

## Description
SpamSentry is the smart spam call and message blocker that protects your peace. Built with Apple's CallKit and MessageFilter frameworks, it delivers system-level blocking that works even when the app is closed.

KEY FEATURES:

Community Blocklist
Automatically sync with a curated database of known spam numbers. Updated regularly to catch the latest robocall campaigns and scam operations.

Custom Blocklist
Add specific phone numbers to your personal blocklist with custom labels. Manage your blocked numbers with ease using search and swipe-to-delete.

Area Code Blocking
Block entire area codes known for spam activity. One tap to silence all numbers from suspicious area codes.

Contact Protection
Never accidentally block someone you know. SpamSentry automatically protects your contacts from being added to the blocklist.

Temporary Pause
Expecting an important call from an unknown number? Pause blocking for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour with one tap.

Extension Health Monitor
Real-time monitoring of your CallKit extension status. Get alerts when the extension needs attention and repair it with a single tap.

Number Search
Quickly check if a phone number is in the blocklist. See whether it was blocked by the community database or your custom rules.

Dashboard Stats
Track how many calls have been blocked with daily and total statistics. See your protection status at a glance.

PRIVACY FIRST:

SpamSentry does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your information stays on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

HOW IT WORKS:

1. Download SpamSentry from the App Store
2. Enable SpamSentry in Phone Settings under Call Blocking and Identification
3. Enable SpamSentry in Messages Settings under Unknown and Spam
4. Sync the community blocklist
5. Enjoy a spam-free phone experience

SpamSentry works at the system level using Apple's CallKit framework, so blocked calls are silenced before your phone even rings. Message filtering uses the IdentityLookup framework to automatically sort junk messages into a separate list.
